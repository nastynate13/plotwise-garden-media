Grouping and separating plants

Real Plotwise recordings. Each clip plays only when you press Play. No sound is needed.

Create a group

  1. Turn on Group plants while placing garlic.
  2. Close the explanation and add four squares, in two separate areas.
  3. Tap the checkmark. All four squares are selected as one planting.

Separate part of a group

  1. Select the group and tap Separate plants.
  2. Select the two squares on the right and check that their size and shape are valid.
  3. Tap the checkmark. Each pair can now be selected independently, with its existing plan retained.
